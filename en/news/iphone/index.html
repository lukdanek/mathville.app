Mathville on iPhone

Mathville now plays on iPhone too, in landscape and with the same sums. The full game only needs buying once.

From version 1.0.3 you can download Mathville to an iPhone as well. It is the same game as on iPad, scaled to the smaller screen, and it plays in landscape.

The Stone Age is free on iPhone too. The full game only needs buying once: on the iPhone you restore it with the same Apple ID, or through Family Sharing. Progress stays on each device, though, so on the iPhone a child starts a village of their own.
