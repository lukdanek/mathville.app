The time machine

Finish all six ages and you can build a time machine in the Future, then go back to any age.

The game doesn't end after the last Trial of the Age: a time machine can now be built in the Future. Upgrade every building to the top level and complete five steps, and the machine takes your child to any age, for expeditions, the villagers' wishes and the Trial of the Age again. Every trip through time looks a little different.

Now and then the advisers of past ages call for help, too: repair the settlement after a storm, fight off a raid, put out a fire or find a lost villager. Each call is a few sums in another setting, so a finished game still has more to give.
