Trophies for stars

The stars earned with sums now win five trophies in every age, and the age's seal.

The stars children collect for right answers now have a use: every age has five trophies to win, from a mammoth tusk in the Stone Age to a starship in the Future. A trophy is won with a short run of sums without a mistake, and its stars are only spent if the run succeeds.

All five trophies of an age earn its seal, which also shows in the chronicle. Trophies don't unlock or block anything: they are a shelf of rewards for extra effort.
