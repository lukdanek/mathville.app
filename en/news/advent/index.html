An Advent calendar in Mathville

From 1 December a door lights up on a winter island every morning: a few sums at your child's level and a present for the village.

From 1 to 24 December Mathville has a winter island with 24 doors, and one more lights up every morning. Behind each door is a short walk of five to eight sums at exactly your child's level, then a present for their village: snow, a snowman, lanterns, a frozen pond with skaters, Christmas cookies and, on Christmas Eve, a Christmas tree with its star.

The calendar is free for every player, including those who only play the free ages. Nothing is lost: a missed door stays lit and can be opened later, until 6 January. There is no clock, and a wrong answer costs nothing.

Every child on the iPad has their own calendar. All 24 doors earn a winter seal that stays in the chronicle after the holidays.
