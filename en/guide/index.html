How to play

Everything a player, or a parent, needs to know. Mathville is played on iPad, held sideways.

Starting a game

On the title screen, tap New game, type a name and choose one of eight heroes. Continue takes you back to the game played last. Up to six games can be kept, one for each player.

The village

Across the top you see your health (hearts), food, stones, points and coins. At the bottom the village's adviser says what to do next. Tap a building to see what it does and what its next level costs.

Building and upgrading

Upgrading a building costs coins and a round of sums. Some buildings give you something: the Forge makes weapons, and the Bakery makes every meal restore more health. A building keeps its old look until you upgrade it in a new age.

The bag

Open the Bag to eat when your hearts run low and to choose which weapon to carry. A stronger weapon hits harder in hunts and fights. The stones you have dug up are kept here too.

Training and the Minute Challenge

Training is calm practice: no foes and no clock, just tasks and a few coins. Challenge opens the Minute Challenge: how many can you solve in one minute? It is the only part of the game with a clock, and you choose when to play it.

Expeditions

Tap Expedition to set out along a trail. Its stops are hunts, fights, a dig site and a trading post; tap Next to walk on to the next one. What you catch hangs at your hero's side, and a flag springs up at every stop you finish.

Hunts and fights

Every right answer is a blow with your weapon, and a quick answer is a critical hit. A hunted animal gives meat and coins; a beaten fighter is blasted away over the hills. When an animal strikes or a fighter hits back you lose a little health, so eat when you need to. You can always leave.

Digging and trading

At a dig site every right answer is one stone. At a trading post you sell stones and meat for coins, or buy food for the road.

Quests and wishes

Each age has eleven quests, laid out as a path to the boss's lair; the adviser always shows the one at hand. Now and then a villager has a wish, such as driving an enemy away. Granting it earns a few coins, and ignoring it costs nothing.

The Trial of the Age

At the end of the quests waits the age's boss. Beat it and a new age begins: a ring of light turns the island into the next era, from the Stone Age through Ancient Times, the Middle Ages, the Industrial Age and Modern Times to the Future. Tap the age's name at the top left to open the Chronicle, with every age you have been through and the boss you beat there.

For parents

Tap the gear at the top right and pass the grown-up check (multiplying two two-digit numbers). There you can set each of the four ladders by hand or let the game adjust them, choose a time limit for hunts and fights (off by default), open How it's going, and read the privacy policy.

The full game

The Stone Age is free to play in full, its boss included. To go on, a grown-up unlocks the full game once, in the parents' panel or at the end of the Stone Age. The purchase is shared with your family through Family Sharing and can be restored on any of your iPads.
