Mathville for teachers

An iPad game in which children build a village with right answers. For a class, the Stone Age is enough, and it is free in full; teachers get the full game from us.

What children practise

Four ladders of small steps. After a run of right answers the game adds a step, after mistakes it quietly drops one, so a child gets about four tasks in five right. Behind the grown-up check you can set each ladder by hand, for instance to what your class is working on.

It suits children from about 6 (sums within 20) to 10 (multiplying and dividing beyond the tables), and most of all those learning their times tables.

Adding and subtracting
Within 10, within 20 with and without bridging ten, within 100, across 100 and up to the thousands.
Times tables
First 2, 5 and 10, then 3 and 4, then 6 to 9. After that, multiples of ten and two-digit numbers times one digit.
Division
Within the tables in the same order, then dividing two-digit numbers.
Missing numbers
Tasks with a box, such as □ + 37 = 95.

In class and at home

Up to six children on one iPad.
Each has their own game with a name and a hero, on school iPads too.
No pressure.
There is no clock unless a grown-up turns it on, and a wrong answer never shows the hero failing.
No internet, accounts or ads.
The game collects nothing about children and sends nothing anywhere, so you can recommend it without worrying about personal data. Privacy policy
